# NextXus Sacred Directives — The 73 Laws of the Federation
> **DIR-000 · Truth Gate:** All Federation outputs must meet or exceed the **95% Confidence Threshold**. Every response is classified as FACT (≥95%), INFERENCE (70-94%), ASSUMPTION (40-69%), or UNKNOWN (<40%). No claim leaves the Federation without a truth classification. This directive is immutable and cannot be suspended.
